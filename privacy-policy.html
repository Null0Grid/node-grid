NODE_GRID Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Short version: NODE_GRID has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing about you or what you do is sent to the developer. What the app keeps, it keeps on your own PC.

What stays on your PC

  • History: only the names of sites you visited (like "example.com"), never full addresses, pages or searches. At most 100 sites, deleted after 7 days. You can clear it any time.
  • Favorites, pinned tabs, loadouts and settings that you create.
  • Saved logins (the key button), encrypted by Windows for your Windows user account, optionally behind a PIN.
  • Pages you save to the Vault (an offline copy and, for articles, a Markdown file).
  • Your Null Drive address and access token, if you connect one in Settings. The token is encrypted by Windows for your Windows user account. Disconnecting deletes both.
  • Files you download from your Null Drive, saved to your Downloads folder.
  • Remembered permissions and trusted local devices that you approve.
  • Browsing cookies and site data are kept in memory only and are erased when you close NODE_GRID. Burner tabs erase theirs when their timer ends. The AI side panel keeps its own separate storage so you stay signed in to the AI service you chose.

When NODE_GRID connects to other services

  • Websites you visit receive the same information any browser sends.
  • Search: searches from the start page go to the search engine you pick. With "Vault" on, they go to search.nullgrid.dev. If you connected a Null Drive, NODE_GRID also sends the search directly to that Drive (usually on your own PC or home network); the Drive address, token and results are never sent to search.nullgrid.dev or to the developer.
  • Null Drive: only if you connect one, NODE_GRID contacts the address you entered when it starts, when you search with Vault on, and when you open or download a file from it.
  • Ad blocking: public block lists are downloaded from Ghostery's servers and cached on your PC.
  • Translate: only when you press translate, the page's address is sent to Google Translate.
  • Radio: when the app starts, it briefly checks whether the built-in radio stations are reachable, and when you play one it streams from that station's servers.
  • AI side panel: loads the AI website you choose. The built-in local AI chat talks only to the server address you enter.
  • Developer tools (Recon): only when you open Recon, DNS lookups are made for that site, including a lookup of who owns its network.

QR codes, reader mode, password generation and page conversion happen entirely on your PC.

Children

NODE_GRID is a general-purpose web browser and is not directed at children under 13.
